01 Background

Line 3 produces titanium brackets for a Tier 1 aerospace customer. The customer flagged an increase in receiving inspection rejections in their May quality report.

Continued non-conformance risks a supplier corrective action request (SCAR) and impacts our on-time delivery score.

03 Goal / Target

Reduce defect rate to ≤ 2.5% by July 15, 2026, sustained for 3 consecutive weeks.

04 Root Cause Analysis

5 Whys applied to: Hole alignment off on 2nd shift, Machine 7.

Why 1 Fixture position drifts during the shift.
Why 2 Locking pin isn't fully engaging.
Why 3 Pin wear exceeds spec, not caught in PM checks.
Why 4 PM checklist doesn't include pin wear measurement — only visual check.
Root Checklist was copied from an older machine model that used a different fixture design.
Root Cause PM checklist for Machine 7 was never updated for the current fixture design, so pin wear is not being measured — only visually inspected.
Fishbone confirmed no other contributing factors. Manpower, Material, and Measurement categories were reviewed and ruled out. Machine and Method were the only verified contributors.
